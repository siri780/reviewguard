Check 4 - Transductive graph setting (explicit disclosure):
  Graph structure (edges, degree, PageRank, components) is computed on the FULL
  45,954-review graph before the train/test split is applied. This is standard
  practice for graph-based fraud detection (transductive setting: the graph

  matches how the original YelpChi graph benchmarks are evaluated. It is NOT
  equivalent to leaking test labels - no label is used in any graph feature -
  but it is weaker than a fully inductive setup where test nodes' edges are

  M3/M4 results as inductive-generalization numbers.
